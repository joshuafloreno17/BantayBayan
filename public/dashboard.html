Flood and Evacuation Safety Guide

During Flooding

  • Move to higher and safer ground when instructed.
  • Never walk or drive through moving floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical wires and flooded outlets.
  • Bring essential medicines and emergency supplies.
  • Monitor official announcements and flood warnings.

During Evacuation

  • Follow instructions from authorized responders.
  • Bring identification and important documents.
  • Prepare water, food, medicine, and a flashlight.
  • Assist children, older persons, and persons with disabilities.
  • Do not return home until authorities declare it safe.

Emergency contact information is based on the Santa Cruz government portal. Contact details and evacuation arrangements should be reconfirmed with the municipality.
